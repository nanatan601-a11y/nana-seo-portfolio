Strategy & Execution

策略与执行

  • Used GSC and GA4 to identify high-potential commercial pages.
  • Optimized page content, TDK, URLs, HTML, schema, and internal links.
  • Built a weekly SEO monitoring workflow for traffic, keywords, pages, and conversion issues.
  • Reviewed Google Ads search terms and conversion paths.
  • Added negative keywords to reduce low-quality inquiries.
  • Connected SEO data, ad data, and landing page performance to improve lead quality.
  • 使用 GSC 和 GA4 找出高潜力商业页面。
  • 优化页面内容、TDK、URL、HTML、schema 和内链。
  • 建立每周 SEO 巡检流程，跟踪流量、关键词、页面和转化问题。
  • 复盘 Google Ads 搜索词和转化路径。
  • 添加否定关键词，减少低质量询盘。
  • 结合 SEO、广告和落地页表现，提升询盘质量。

After optimization, monthly Google Ads spend was approximately $5.7K; a newly created ad group generated 7 qualified leads.

优化后 Google Ads 月度支出约为 $5.7K；新建广告组获得 7 个有效线索。
