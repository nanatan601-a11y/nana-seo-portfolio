Building a multi-channel local search growth system.

建立多平台协同的本地搜索增长体系。

Seattle real estate customers use search engines, local platforms, and community discussions to research the market and evaluate agents. I connected localized content, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Reddit, and Yelp to build consistent local visibility, professional credibility, and customer trust throughout the decision journey.

西雅图房地产客户会通过搜索引擎、本地平台和社区讨论研究市场并筛选经纪人。我围绕客户决策路径，将地域化内容、Google Business Profile、Google Ads、Reddit 和 Yelp 连接起来，持续建立本地可见度、专业可信度和客户信任。

Workflow

From local demand research to qualified leads.

从本地需求研究到有效线索。

01

Business Problem

Real estate is location-specific and has a long decision cycle. A website alone could not build visibility and trust across fragmented research journeys.

02

Market Diagnosis

I researched the platforms, search terms, and real questions used by Seattle buyers at different stages of their decisions.

03

Strategic Direction

The brand needed consistent local expertise, market data, and differentiation across the platforms buyers already used.

04

Optimization

I connected localized website content, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Reddit, and Yelp around real buyer concerns.

05

Validation & Learning

CRM results measured lead quality, while Reddit discussions supplied authentic customer language for future content and campaigns.

01

业务问题

房地产决策周期长、地域性强，客户研究分散在多个平台，仅依靠网站难以持续建立可见度和信任。

02

市场诊断

研究西雅图买家在不同决策阶段使用的平台、搜索词和真实问题。

03

策略判断

需要在客户常用的平台持续展示一致的地域经验、市场数据和品牌优势。

04

优化执行

围绕真实买家疑虑，联动网站、Google Business Profile、Google Ads、Reddit 和 Yelp。

05

结果验证与复盘

通过 CRM 验证线索质量，并将 Reddit 真实讨论用于后续内容与广告优化。
