Tracing invalid leads back to wasted ad spend.

Moka SFX's advertising conversions included a significant number of inquiries unrelated to its products. I traced invalid leads back to their sources and built a continuously updated exclusion system across Search, Display, and Video campaigns. Monthly ad spend fell by approximately 33% while qualified lead volume and quality remained stable.

My Role: B2B SEO & SEM Strategist Market: International B2B Stage Equipment Period: May–September 2025 Data Source: Google Ads & lead inquiry records

Workflow

From lead feedback to campaign exclusions.

01

Business Problem

Inquiries about app development, gaming, support services, and other unrelated requests wasted budget and increased sales screening.

02

Data Diagnosis

I classified invalid leads, traced their campaign sources, and reviewed search terms, locations, audiences, websites, apps, and placements.

03

Cause Assessment

Invalid traffic was distributed across campaign types without a continuously updated negative-keyword and placement-exclusion system.

04

Optimization

I used AI-assisted classification in Google Sheets and Looker Studio, then excluded irrelevant terms, websites, apps, and placements.

05

Validation & Learning

Qualified leads remained stable as spend fell, creating a repeatable feedback loop between lead quality and campaign settings.

Measured Outcome

Lower spend without sacrificing qualified leads.

AI supported the classification process; the core improvement was the feedback loop connecting real inquiry quality with advertising sources and campaign exclusions.
